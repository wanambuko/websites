Modesty

Modesty means behaving, dressing, or speaking in a humble and respectful way without showing off or seeking too much attention.


It can apply in different ways:

  • Character/personality:

    • A modest person does not boast about achievements or abilities.

  • Clothing:

    • Dressing in a decent and respectful manner.

  • Behavior:

    • Acting with humility and self-control.

    Example sentences:

    1. “She was modest even after winning the competition.”
    2. “Many cultures encourage modest dressing.”
    • Modesty
      • Humility
      • Respectful dressing
      • Kind speech
      • Good behavior
    • Benefits of Modesty
      1. Earns respect
      2. Shows self-control
      3. Reflects good character
